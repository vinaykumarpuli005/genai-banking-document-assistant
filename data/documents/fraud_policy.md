# Synthetic Fraud Monitoring Policy

Document ID: SYN-BANK-003
Effective date: 2026-03-01

## Transaction monitoring
Transactions may be flagged when they match predefined fraud-risk rules.

## Customer verification
When a transaction is flagged, additional customer verification may be required before the transaction is completed.

## False positives
Fraud alerts should be reviewed using available transaction context. Analysts should document the reason for clearing or escalating an alert.

## Escalation
High-risk cases should be escalated according to the applicable fraud operations procedure.

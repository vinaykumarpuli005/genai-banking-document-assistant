# Synthetic Debit Card Policy

Document ID: SYN-BANK-001
Effective date: 2026-01-01

## ATM withdrawal limit
The standard debit card has a maximum ATM cash withdrawal limit of INR 50,000 per day.

## International transactions
International ATM and point-of-sale transactions may require international usage to be enabled on the card.

## Card blocking
Customers should immediately report a lost or stolen card through the bank's official support channel.
